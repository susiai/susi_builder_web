Table help F2 Edit cell · Shift + arrow to extend selection

Double-click a cell to edit. Click a heading to select rows or columns. Drag a column header or row number to move it. Drag the selection handle to extend a series. Drag a column edge to resize or double-click it to fit. Start a formula with =, for example =SUM(A1:A5) or =WENN(B1>0;B1;0). Use PI or E as a constant, for example =LN(E). Choose a suggestion with the arrow keys and Tab or Enter. Press F4 on a reference to cycle through B2, $B$2, B$2 and $B2. For a range, select one endpoint to change it separately. Trigonometric functions take angles in radians. For degrees, use =SIN(RADIANS(30)). When a formula expects a value, click a cell or drag across cells to insert a range. The colors in the formula bar match the outlines on the sheet. Open formula help with F1 or the ƒx ? button. Join text with =A1 & " " & B1. Use =TEXTJOIN(", ",TRUE,A1:A5) to separate nonempty cells with a comma and a space. =LLM(A1) asks the configured chat model when the question changes. XLSX keeps the formula and answer. From a selected cell, Alt + ←/→ moves its column and Alt + ↑/↓ moves its row.
